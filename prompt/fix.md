# WHAT THE VIEWER MUST UNDERSTAND

By the end, the viewer should understand these five things without needing a technical explanation:

### 01 — Current AI

AI can produce outputs that *look* like work.

But often:

> **Nothing actually happens.**

### 02 — Connectors

Traditional AI employees depend on integrations.

> **Every new tool requires another connector.**

### 03 — Bench

Bench gives workers actual computers.

> **If a human can use the software, the worker can use it too.**

### 04 — Company

Bench isn't just an engineering agent.

A company can have workers for:

**Engineering · Research · Marketing · Sales · Operations · Finance · Customer Success**

and other functions.

### 05 — Control

Workers aren't permanent autonomous entities.

They are:

**hired → equipped → supervised → verified → dismissed.**

The company persists.

The workers don't have to.
